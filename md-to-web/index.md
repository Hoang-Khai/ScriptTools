## Reading passage:

### Master of Disguise

Octopuses are famous for their round bodies, big eyes, and eight arms. There are many different types of octopuses, but all are alike in one way: They are masters[^1] of **disguise**. Octopuses can change their **appearance** in less than a second to look like rocks, plants, or even other animals. How do they do this?

An octopus can disguise itself in three ways. One is by using color. An octopus's skin has special cells[^2] called chromatophores. These cells are filled with yellow, brown, and red pigment[^3]. When an octopus moves its muscles a certain way, the cells become large and produce colorful spots and other **patterns** on its skin. Chromatophores can also **reflect** light. In blue light, for example, an octopus's skin will look blue. In white light, its skin will look white. With these cells, an octopus can produce many different skin colors and patterns.

An octopus can also change its skin **texture**[^4]. When the octopus moves its muscles, its skin can go from smooth to spiky. It might then look like a plant, or coral. Another way an octopus disguises itself is by changing its shape. Some, for example, roll their bodies into balls so they look like rocks. One type of octopus can change its form to look like other sea creatures-especially dangerous ones, such as sea snakes.

Why are octopuses so good at disguising themselves? They have to be. The ocean is not a safe place for them. Because they have no bones in their bodies, octopuses are like large pieces of meat. Many **predators** want to eat them-and they can eat them whole. To **survive**, octopuses have developed the amazing ability to change their appearance very quickly in order to hide from predators.

---

**Footnotes:**
* [^1] **master**: A master is very good at doing something.
* [^2] **cell**: A cell is the smallest living part of an animal or plant. Most animals have billions of cells in their bodies.
* [^3] **pigment**: Pigment is a substance that gives something color. For example, green pigment makes most plants look green.
* [^4] **texture**: Texture is how something looks and feels (e.g., soft, smooth, spiky).

## Answer Key

**Question 1**
- **Correct Answer:** A
- **Explanation:** Ý chính của bài đọc là giải thích cách bạch tuộc sử dụng các phương pháp ngụy trang (đổi màu, kết cấu da, hình dáng) để trốn tránh kẻ thù và sinh tồn trong đại dương.

**Question 2**
- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Explanation:** Thông tin chi tiết ở đoạn 2: 'When an octopus moves its muscles a certain way, the cells become large and produce colorful spots and other patterns on its skin.'

**Question 3**
- **Correct Answer:** D
- **Explanation:** Phương án D không được nhắc tới. Bài đọc chỉ đề cập đến việc bạch tuộc đổi màu sắc, đổi kết cấu da và thay đổi hình dáng cơ thể.

**Question 4**
- **Correct Answer:** C
- **Explanation:** Đoạn 4 nêu rõ: 'Because they have no bones in their bodies, octopuses are like large pieces of meat. Many predators want to eat them...'. Do đó có thể suy ra cơ thể mềm không có xương khiến chúng rất dễ bị tấn công.

**Question 5**
- **Correct Answer:** B
- **Explanation:** Tác giả nhắc đến 'sea snakes' để làm ví dụ minh họa cho khả năng thay đổi hình dáng cơ thể của bạch tuộc nhằm bắt chước các sinh vật nguy hiểm khác.
